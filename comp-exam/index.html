What is hypertension? It is the medical name for high blood pressure: the pressure of the blood inside the blood vessels stays higher than normal for a long time.

Blood pressure has two numbers. The upper number, systolic, is the pressure when the heart beats. The lower number, diastolic, is the pressure when the heart rests.

RQ1 · Care at home

Two trial plans bring heart care into the home.

Stepped-wedge randomized trial (protocol)

Adults with heart failure, Gallup Indian Medical Center and Tohatchi Health Clinic, Navajo Nation (the Hózhó trial).

Five groups cross over every 30 days, so every patient receives the model. A study team starts and adjusts medicines by phone, using readings from a home blood pressure cuff given to every patient. [1]

370 urban American Indian and Alaska Native adults planned, at three urban Indian clinics in Washington and Oklahoma (protocol).

RQ1: What counts as playful health technology?

Serious game

A game whose main purpose is not entertainment, such as health [1]. Example: eSMART, Heart Health Mountain

Gamification

Game elements, such as points and rewards, added to a task that is not a game [2]. Example: Rewards for logging blood pressure [3]

Playification
